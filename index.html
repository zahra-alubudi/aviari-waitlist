07 · Questions

You asked, we answered.

What actually happens after I join the waitlist?+
You lock your spot. Between now and launch, I'll send you useful revision materials. Then you get first access when doors open.
Do I still need a flight school or ground school?+
You'll sit the official CAA/EASA exams through your flight school as normal. That doesn't change. Aviari is how you make sure you walk in ready to pass them, first time, on a schedule.
Is 12 weeks realistic? I have a job.+
Twelve weeks is the pace, and it's ambitious by design. This is intensive: you won't get through it without sacrifices, and yes, that means missing a few pints and skipping a few date nights. Getting a pilot licence is not easy. Lock in for twelve weeks and get it done.
I've already passed some exams. Is this still for me?+
Yes. Aviari comes as a full package covering all nine subjects, or as individual subjects. Either way you get a 12-week plan you can adapt around what you've already passed.
I've already failed one. Will this help?+
Yes, absolutely. You have four attempts per exam, so one fail is a setback, not the end. The plan rebuilds around your retake, you drill the weak points, and this time you walk in knowing you're ready.
How much will it cost?+
The waitlist prices above are exactly what you'll pay at launch: locked at signup, one-off, no subscription. It will never be cheaper than it is for the people on this list.
UK CAA or EASA?+
Both. The curriculum is targeted at the UK CAA and EASA syllabi, which share the same nine-subject structure, so it works whichever authority you're training under.
